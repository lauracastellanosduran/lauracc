## What they design / produce

Design-led aluminium **day-boat** brand. Named for founder Isaac Burrough’s birth year. Public pitch: European day-cruiser finish + grit for a southerly in the Sounds. Starts with the designer’s pencil, not a welder’s jig — plumb bow, crisp surfaces, alloy that is not supposed to “scream tinny.”

Hull One is in daily Sounds use (~6.4 m, seating for ~10, 21° deadrise; owners have pushed it near 90 km/h on a Mercury V8). **Hull Two** in build for early 2026, public debut planned for the **Auckland Boat Show**: lower floor, simpler/lighter structure, U-lounge bow, electric targa that drops to the windscreen. Helm is a single 12-inch Simrad + Mercury SmartCraft; Zipwake/Humphree as built-in options.
