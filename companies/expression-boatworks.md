## What they build / design

Father-and-son GRP trailer fishing boats with American sportfisher styling: **390T**, **400SC**, **400CC**, **430CC/SC** (3.9–4.3 m, 40–50 hp). Solid fibreglass hull and deck; beach-launchable. NZ Marine: “high-performance fibreglass fishing vessels.” Design is in-house (founders), not a studio with an ID bench.

## Fit for Igor

**Near-miss (score 6).** They do design and produce boats, and styling is the brand — but the product is a tiny tiller/console fisherman, founder-built, no hire signal. Wrong scale vs Aeolos / DEOND.
