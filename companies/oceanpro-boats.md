## What they design / produce

In-house designed aluminium pontoon trailer boats for NZ conditions: centre consoles (4.5 m, 4.6 m EVO, **535CC**), plus cuddy/hardtop models ~5–5.3 m. Signature sheerline and tapered pontoons (elevated bow plate, extra reserve buoyancy). 535CC: 55° entry, demo for Waikato Boat/Fish/Dive show; Honda 75–90 hp. Also repairs, mods, trailers. Dealers: Family Boats (Auckland), Peninsula Marine, Bay Marine, Manmarine (AU).
