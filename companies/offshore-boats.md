## What they design / produce

Whangārei alloy plate-boat producer. Founder Jonathan Barlow started the brand because he would not sell another trailer boat he did not believe in. Monohull range (650 / 750 / 850 HT and Circa-named trailer models) designed with naval architect **Alan Walker** and Perin Clark — 8 mm plate, 18° deadrise, fishing/diving hardtops. Consecutive Supreme Boat of the Show wins (incl. 850HT / Circa 950 HT).

**New product line (2025–26):** foil-assisted aluminium powercats by **Dan Leech**. 7.35 m delivered mid-2025; 1100 HTC (~11 m, 4 m beam, 6 mm hull, twin 600–1200 hp, ~32 kn cruise) premiered at the 2026 Auckland Boat Show; 9 m ready to build; 14 m on the horizon. Boating NZ review: [Offshore 1100 HTC](https://www.boatingnz.co.nz/boat-reviews/offshore-foil-assisted-powercat/).
