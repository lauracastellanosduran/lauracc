## What they build / design

Northland alloy fabrication shop. Owner **Luke Seuren** (sheet-metal trade) is listed as Head Designer; wife Kelly is Business Manager. Custom aluminium commercial/recreational vessels: passenger ferries, water taxis, oyster barges, Hauraki Gulf delivery barges, Whanganui transport barges, commercial fishing. Also ute decks / service bodies. Small team (Dean Busher, Hone Kingi named).
