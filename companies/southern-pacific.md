## What they build / design

NZ’s main **inflatable / RIB producer** (since 1988; factory Kumeu from 2010). Range: yacht-club chase boats (Cormorant, SeekR, PerformR), tenders (X-Tender, Shore Tender, Prodigy luxury), aluminium tenders (Puffin, Shearwater), **Club Electric 490** with ZeroJet, plus Hypalon tubes to 14 m for coastguard and military contracts. Site: “New Zealand Designed.” About page: hull/product evolution uses **external local naval architects**; factory does digital pattern cutting, thermal welding, CNC alloy hulls.

Parent **Kiwi Yachting Ltd** (Richard Macalister, Isabel Macalister, Dean Barker as directors) also owns AutoAnchor and Safety at Sea — those arms are not ID targets.
