## What they build

Amphibious RIBs and alloy craft, plus amphibious enablement kits for other builders. Recreational, commercial/rescue, and superyacht-tender use. 1,500+ units in 50+ countries. Range includes 3.8–12 m RIBs, alloy cuddy/cabin models, and a **7.5 m electric amphibious RIB**. Designed and manufactured in Auckland (est. 2004; founders Maurice Bryham and David McKee Wright). US office in Bristol, RI. ~40 people.
