High-performance composite sailing dinghies and foiling craft: Olympic 470 / 49er, youth classes (420, 29er, Starling, Zephyr), Bieker Moth and other foil platforms. Production composites, not custom superyachts. Designs are often class-legal / licensed (e.g. Bieker Moth), built and developed in Silverdale.
