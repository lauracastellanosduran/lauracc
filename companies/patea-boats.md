## What they design / produce

New South Island alloy adventure-boat brand (Pātea Boats Ltd). First model **Pātea 830** (8.3 m, 2.75 m beam, ~4.3 t BMT, Volvo Penta D4-320 sterndrive, 500 L fuel). Marketed for lakes, West Coast swell, Fiordland. Hull by **Hall Marine Design**; cabin, helm and systems customised by the family. Demonstrator nearly complete late 2025 / featured Feb 2026.
