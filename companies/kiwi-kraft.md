## What they build / design

Family aluminium **pontoon** brand (est. 1988, Rodney Harris). NZ Marine listing is **Kiwi Engineering and Marine Ltd** — same business, not a separate repair shop. 17 models (hardtop, cuddy, dinghy, console); flagship 795 HT / HTE. Site: 14-person hand-build team, variable-deadrise hull, positive buoyancy, twin planing strakes, layout customisation. Southern Ocean / Fiordland fishing brief.
