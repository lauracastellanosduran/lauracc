## What they design

New Zealand yacht design studio (founded 2014 by Kit Carlier). **Interior and exterior superyacht design and styling** — not a hydrostatics-only NA shop. Services: space planning / GA, exterior architecture, marine furniture, 3D render/animation, manufacturing drawings and CNC, FF&E, yard supervision. Projects include new-builds and refits (German Frers 57 with McConaghy; Rua Moana; WEBBCo Ika Rere ferry interiors as published news). Kit’s path: composite/wood boatbuilder → 5-axis production (UK) → Mustang Marine production interiors → **lead interior designer at Alloy Yachts** (Kokomo, Vertigo, Loretta Anne, Encore, Mondango) → Odyssey Yacht Design (tenders, military craft, superyacht styling) → own studio.
