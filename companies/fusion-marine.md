## What they build

Family yard (DW Bettany / Fusion Marine Ltd, formerly Fusion Catamarans NZ). Custom builds, refits, repairs, haulout. Wood through **carbon composite**. Public projects: full-carbon 10.5 m race yacht (**designed by Rob Shaw**), Fusion sailing / 48 ft power cats (**Garry Lidgard**), high-speed RIB. Stu: Volvo Ocean Race, America’s Cup, foiling. Do **not** confuse with South African **Fusion Power Boats**.

## Design-team signals

They **build** advanced composites; hull design on cited projects is external (Shaw, Lidgard). No jobs page, no designer ads.
