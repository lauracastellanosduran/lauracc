## What they design / produce

Composite **Wright 52** (~15–16 m) powercats — sedan and flybridge. Originally an America’s Cup photo-boat hull; Kays have built six. Latest flybridge *Catfish* (twin Yanmar 6LY 440 hp, ~18 kn cruise / 28 kn max, GRP, teak, game-fish spec) built for a Whitsundays owner and shown toward Sanctuary Cove. Boating NZ: Kays now concentrate on **marketing and sales**; Robertson’s yard helps **build**.
