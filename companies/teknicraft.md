Independent naval-architecture studio (est. 1996). High-performance commercial and recreational **power catamarans**, including hydrofoil-supported hulls. 130+ foiled vessels in service worldwide. Passenger, survey, patrol, research, motor-yacht. Nic de Waal (FRINA) publicly notes current work on **composite, zero-emission passenger vessels** up to ~30 kn. Designs built by yards in NZ, US (All American Marine), and elsewhere — they are a design house, not a production shed.
