## What they build

Composite **all-electric passenger ferries** (15–22 m). Flagship: Ika Rere — 19 m, 20 kn, ~132 pax carbon-fibre cat for East by West Ferries (Queen’s Wharf–Days Bay). Founded 2018 after Jeremy Ward commissioned the first Southern Hemisphere high-performance electric ferry. MD Fraser Foote: 20 years Northland Contract Boatbuilders + BMW Oracle AC composites.

## Design-team signals

Site says “design team” experience across workboats, superyachts, luxury powerboats, AC yachts — but **naval architecture is SSC Marine** (Stimson + Kit Carlier + Tennant/Stanton). WEBBCo’s core is composite production + design coordination. LinkedIn ~2 named staff. No jobs page.

## Fit for Igor

**Score 6.** Real carbon production, R&D electric — proof-aligned materials — but they are a ferry builder that buys design, not an ID employer. Do not apply unless they advertise styling/product. Jackson CNC’d parts for Ika Rere; that does not make Jackson a boat brand.
