## What they built

Iconic NZ aluminium trailer-boat brand (Trevor Fyfe + Graeme Ranson, 1969). Dinghies to ~7.6 m hardtops (Pursuit GT). Fully welded pressed-plate production. Business offered for sale in **2015** (Bayleys) including Fyran + Parkercraft IP. Power Boat Magazine buyers’ guide treats the brand as **resigned to history**. No NZ Marine pleasure-builder listing, no 2025–26 hiring, no confirmed factory.
