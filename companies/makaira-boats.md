# Makaira Boats

- **Website:** https://www.makairaboats.com/
- **Careers:** https://www.makairaboats.com/careers/ (verified 2026-09-09)
- **Location:** New Zealand (purpose-built factory; founder story starts post–Alloy Yachts 2011)
- **Careers apply:** form on careers page (Paint / Fabrication / Fit Out / Apprenticeship / Other)

## What they build

Aluminium offshore sport-fishing boats with a Carolina-flare hull (claimed world-first in alloy). Range ~25–65 ft, inboard or outboard. Lifetime hull warranty. Factory aimed at ~50 boats/year; lean line. Exports Australia and USA. Founder Allan Shaw drafted hull no.1 (King Bank 940) after leaving Alloy Yachts as marine systems engineer.

## Design-team signals

Founder-led hull/product design. Careers 2026-09-09: **Marine Blaster and Painter** (NZ work rights required, $33.60–$37/hr) plus standing apprentice fabricator / marine electrician–fitout. No designer / ID / draughtsperson vacancy. Form includes “Other.”

## Fit for Igor

In-house boat design exists and they talk performance hulls, but product is alloy game-fishing and live seats are trades. Speculative ID ~6. Visa-hostile on the painter card (must already have NZ work rights). Watch if they ever post a designer; do not apply to paint/fabrication.
