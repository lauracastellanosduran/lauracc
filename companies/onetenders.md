## What they design / produce

Family carbon-tender brand (est. ~2023; public “three years of development”). Grant & Emma Nelson. Autoclaved **prepreg carbon** M-Series monohulls and C-Series cats, petrol or electric. Public models: M35 (~45 kg, 3.5 m, 10–15 hp), C35 (~65–70 kg, 3.5 m), C42 (~120 kg, 4.2 m, to 40 hp, 5–6 pax). Site also lists an M38/C38-class mid-size. Built as a product range (console / tiller / open-deck variants), not one-offs. Sep 2026: opening international dealer talks after locking manufacturing.

Grant Nelson: Director of Engineering; prior Lead Vehicle Engineer at **Vessev**, Principal Product Development at Fisher & Paykel Healthcare. Emma: co-founder / marketing (ex Marsden Maritime Holdings).
