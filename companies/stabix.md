## What they build / design

Independent **design-led amphibious** (and optional non-amphibious) alloy boats, 20–29 ft. Models: 205, 240, 250, 290; Anura or Tectrax wheel systems. Custom fit-out per owner; ~25 boats/year. Spun out of Stabicraft in 2020–21 so founder Paul Adams could get back to design thinking / product development. Still owned separately from Stabicraft production.
