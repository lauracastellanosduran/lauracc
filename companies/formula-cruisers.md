# Formula Cruisers

- **Historic website:** formulacruisers.co.nz (unreliable / 500 in prior runs)
- **Location:** formerly Kumeu / Hobsonville, Auckland
- **Status:** **Closed 2013** — stop chasing

## What they built

GRP production and semi-custom cruisers (Formula 4000/4500; later ICON flybridge range). ICON 54/58 moulds sold to Dean Salthouse / Next Generation Boats. Prowler 10.4 powercats were to move to another Auckland yard.

## Fit for Igor

None. Yard shut after ~30 years (high NZ dollar, export collapse). Directory listings that still show Hudson Bay Rd / Hobsonville are stale. Treat like Cookson: **do not re-research**.

## Research status

Validated closed via contemporary boatsales.com.au report (10 May 2013). Filed 2026-09-09 so the name is not re-opened as “thin.”
