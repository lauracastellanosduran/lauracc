# K2 Marine — closed (liquidation)

- **Historic website:** https://k2marine.co.nz/ (HTTP 500 on 2026-09-21)
- **Historic location:** 7 Culperry Road, Glendene, Auckland 0602
- **Founders:** Kelvin Kay · Amos Kay · sales@k2marine.co.nz

## What they built

West Auckland custom composite luxury cats: **Wright 52** sedan and flybridge (designer **Alan Wright**; Kays owned the moulds). Also advertised Aqua Homes composite houseboat cats and custom builds. Showed at Sanctuary Cove 2025.

## Status

**Voluntary liquidation ~November 2025** (NZ Herald / Reynolds & Associates; Grant Reynolds). ~$2m creditors including >$1.2m owed to the Kays. Site still up in places but not an employer.
