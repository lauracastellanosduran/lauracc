# K2 Marine — closed (liquidation)

- **Historic website:** https://k2marine.co.nz/ (HTTP 500 on 2026-09-21)
- **Historic location:** 7 Culperry Road, Glendene, Auckland 0602
- **Founders:** Kelvin Kay · Amos Kay · sales@k2marine.co.nz

## What they built

West Auckland custom composite luxury cats: **Wright 52** sedan and flybridge (designer **Alan Wright**; Kays owned the moulds). Also advertised Aqua Homes composite houseboat cats and custom builds. Showed at Sanctuary Cove 2025.

## Status

**Voluntary liquidation ~November 2025** (NZ Herald / Reynolds & Associates; Grant Reynolds). ~$2m creditors including >$1.2m owed to the Kays. Site still **HTTP 500**.

**Successor (do not treat as fully dead brand):** Kelvin and Amos Kay reincorporated as **Wright Catamarans Australasia Ltd** (11 Sep 2025) and amalgamated build with **Robertson Boats**, Warkworth. Boating NZ May 2026 Wright 52 Flybridge *Catfish* is this entity. See `wright-catamarans.md` (score 6 — still not an ID employer).

## Fit for Igor

**Do not research K2 again as an employer.** Wright 52 design still sits with Alan Wright (not a hiring studio). Hiring vehicle, if any, would be Wright Catamarans / Robertson — neither has a design seat today. Do not confuse with Kingfisher / Alloy Cats or US K2 Marine.
